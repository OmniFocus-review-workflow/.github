## Why This Exists

Most task tools collect items faster than they help you finish them. Lists grow, priorities blur, and the next action becomes hard to find. **OmniFocus** solves this by building a GTD environment where capture, clarification, organization, and review stay connected.

OmniFocus solves **one specific problem**: turning scattered commitments into trusted next actions. Open OmniFocus, collect what has your attention, process it into projects and contexts, then work from a review workflow that keeps the system honest.

If you are tired of guessing what to do next, **OmniFocus** is for you. The OmniFocus GTD approach keeps every task manager session grounded in clear outcomes, realistic project planning, and a weekly review that actually happens.

OmniFocus is built for people who treat productivity as a practice, not a one-time setup. Every inbox item, project, and perspective supports a repeatable OmniFocus productivity system that grows with your responsibilities instead of collapsing under them.
